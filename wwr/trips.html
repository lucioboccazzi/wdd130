Trip Details

Family Float

A relaxed half-day trip on gentle Class I-II water. Your guide points out wildlife along the banks, and there are plenty of spots to stop for a swim. It is a great first rafting experience for young children and anyone nervous about whitewater.

  • Duration: 3 hours
  • Difficulty: Beginner (Class I-II)
  • Minimum age: 5

Canyon Run

Our most popular full-day trip. You will paddle through steady Class III rapids between tall canyon walls, break for a picnic lunch on a sandy beach, then tackle the afternoon's biggest drops. No experience is needed, just a willingness to get wet.

  • Duration: 6 hours
  • Difficulty: Intermediate (Class III)
  • Minimum age: 12

Two-Day River Expedition

Spend two days on the river with a night of camping in between. You will run Class III-IV rapids, share a campfire dinner cooked by your guides, and sleep under the stars. Tents, sleeping bags, and meals are provided.

  • Duration: 2 days, 1 night
  • Difficulty: Advanced (Class III-IV)
  • Minimum age: 14
